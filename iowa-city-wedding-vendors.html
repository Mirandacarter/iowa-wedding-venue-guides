Iowa City Wedding Vendors

Photographers, caterers, florists, bakers, DJs, officiants, and rental companies that our team has met in person, with typical prices so you can plan with confidence.

How We Choose Vendors

Every vendor on this page has met with our vendor coordinator, Tomas Okafor, and has worked at least three weddings in the Iowa City area. We look for clear pricing, written contracts, prompt replies, and strong reviews from past couples. Vendors cannot pay to be listed here. Vendors marked "Small-wedding package" offer a reduced rate for guest lists under 50.

Questions to Ask Every Vendor

  1. Are you available on our date, and how long will you hold it before we sign?
  2. What exactly is included in the price, and what costs extra?
  3. How much is the deposit, and when is the final payment due?
  4. What happens if you are sick or have an emergency on our wedding day?
  5. Have you worked at our venue before?
  6. Can we see a full wedding you did, not just the best highlights?
Tomas's advice: Get every promise in writing. If a vendor says setup is included or that they will stay an extra hour, make sure it appears in the contract before you pay the deposit.
