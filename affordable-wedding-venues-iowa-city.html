Affordable Wedding Venues in Iowa City

Five budget-friendly spaces in and around Iowa City, each with a real price range, guest capacity, and a plain-language note on what you actually get for the money.

What "Affordable" Means in This Guide

The venue is usually the single largest line in a wedding budget. For this guide, we only included spaces where a Saturday rental costs $2,500 or less, and where Friday, Sunday, or off-season dates bring the price down even further. Prices below are for the 2027 wedding season and were confirmed by our vendor coordinator, Tomas Okafor, during in-person visits.

Our Top Affordable Venue Picks

Linden Hall Community Room

Downtown Iowa City · Up to 120 guests

$650 Friday or Sunday · $950 Saturday

A bright, high-ceilinged hall with tall windows and wood floors. Tables, chairs, and a full catering kitchen are included, and you can bring any caterer you like.

Best for: couples who want a blank space they can decorate their own way.

Willowmere Pavilion

Riverside park, south Iowa City · Up to 150 guests

$400 per day (May through October)

An open-air shelter beside the river with built-in string lights, picnic tables, and a lawn for the ceremony. Restrooms and electrical outlets are on site.

Best for: relaxed outdoor weddings on a tight budget. Plan a rain backup with a tent rental.

The Old Mill Loft

North Iowa City · Up to 180 guests

$1,100 off-peak · $1,800 Saturday in season

A restored grain building with exposed brick, timber beams, and a second-floor loft for cocktails. Includes 20 round tables, chairs, and two hours of setup time the day before.

Best for: an industrial-chic look without the downtown hotel price.

Coralville Arts Annex

Coralville · Up to 90 guests

$900 any day of the week

A working art gallery that rents its main room in the evenings. The rotating local artwork means your decorations are already on the walls.

Best for: creative couples who want a unique backdrop with very little decorating.

Sycamore Ridge Farm

15 minutes south of Iowa City · Up to 200 guests

$1,500 Friday · $2,400 Saturday

A white barn with a gravel courtyard, fire pit, and an orchard ceremony site. The price includes setup and teardown by the farm staff, plus a bridal suite in the farmhouse.

Best for: larger guest lists and couples who want a classic rustic barn wedding.

Five Ways to Save on Your Venue

  1. Choose a Friday or Sunday. Most venues in this guide charge 30 to 40 percent less than on a Saturday.
  2. Consider the off-season. November through March dates are often the cheapest of the year, and winter light makes for lovely indoor photos.
  3. Hold the ceremony and reception in one place. You save on a second rental fee and on transportation for guests.
  4. Ask what is included. A cheaper venue can cost more in the end if you have to rent every table and chair separately.
  5. Read the cleanup rules. Some venues charge a fee if you leave late or do not remove decorations, so build that time into your plan.
Sample budget from Priya: A 100-guest Friday wedding at Linden Hall can come in around $11,500 total: $650 venue, $3,800 catering, $2,400 photography, $1,200 flowers, $900 DJ, $450 cake, $1,100 rentals and decor, and $1,000 for attire and extras.
