Affordable Wedding Planning in the Iowa City Area

Riverbend Vows is a local planning guide for couples who want a wedding that feels personal, looks lovely, and still leaves room in the budget for the honeymoon.

Why We Started Riverbend Vows

Founder Maren Lindqvist planned her own Iowa City wedding in 2019 on a budget of just under $9,000. She spent months calling venues, comparing quotes, and learning which "packages" were actually a good deal. When friends started asking for her spreadsheet, she realized other couples needed the same help. Riverbend Vows grew out of that spreadsheet.

Today our small team visits venues in person, talks with local vendors, and writes honest guides that show real price ranges up front. We do not charge couples for our guides, and every recommendation explains who the option is best for, not just why it is nice.

How We Help Couples Save

Real Prices Up Front

Every venue and vendor listing shows a price range, so you can rule things out quickly instead of waiting days for a quote.

Off-Peak Know-How

A Friday in November can cost half as much as a Saturday in June. We point out which dates and seasons offer the best value.

Local Connections

We know which caterers will work in a park shelter and which florists offer small-wedding bundles, so you are not starting from scratch.

Quick tip: Set your guest count before you tour a single venue. Guest count drives almost every other cost, from catering to chairs to cake, and knowing it first keeps you from falling in love with a space that is too big or too small.
