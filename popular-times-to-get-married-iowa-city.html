Most Popular Times to Get Married in Iowa City

Which months, days, and times of day book up first in Iowa City, what that means for your budget, and the local calendar dates every couple should check before choosing a wedding date.

The Short Answer

Across the 212 weddings our team helped plan from 2022 through 2026, the most popular choice was a Saturday in June or September, with a ceremony starting between 4 and 5 p.m. Those dates also cost the most and sell out the fastest. Many of the best venues in our guides book their prime Saturdays 12 to 18 months ahead.

The good news is that popular does not mean best. Couples who choose a slightly less popular date often get the same venue, the same vendors, and the same beautiful photos for much less money.

Most Popular Months

Iowa City has two clear wedding peaks: early summer and early fall. Late spring can bring heavy rain, and July and August can be hot and humid, so many couples aim for June or the cooler weeks of September and October.

Most Popular Days of the Week

Saturday

68% of our weddings

The easiest day for guests to attend, which is why it costs the most. Expect to pay full price at nearly every venue and vendor.

Friday

18% of our weddings

The fastest-growing choice. Guests can take a half day off work, and most venues charge 30 to 40 percent less than on Saturday.

Sunday

11% of our weddings

A great fit for brunch or afternoon weddings. Holiday weekends, when guests have Monday off, are the most popular Sundays.

Weekdays

3% of our weddings

The biggest savings of all, and a good choice for small weddings and elopements where most guests live nearby.

Most Popular Times of Day

  • Late afternoon (4 to 5 p.m.) is the favorite. It allows a ceremony, photos, and dinner to flow straight into an evening reception.
  • Golden-hour ceremonies are popular in September and October. Sunset arrives earlier in fall, so photographers often suggest starting around 4 p.m. to catch the warm light.
  • Morning and brunch weddings are a smart way to save. Caterers often charge less for brunch menus, and venues may offer a lower rate for daytime rentals.
  • Evening-only events suit small weddings. A 6 p.m. ceremony followed by dinner works well at spaces like The Juniper Room.

Local Dates to Check Before You Decide

Iowa City is a college town, and a few big weekends affect hotel prices, traffic, and restaurant availability across the whole area.
